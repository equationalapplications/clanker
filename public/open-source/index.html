What does Open Source mean for you?

Most AI companies operate in a black box — you have to trust marketing promises about privacy and data handling without ever seeing what happens behind the scenes. Clanker AI is an open book. Our entire app is published on GitHub, which aligns with our core values: local-first memory, user-owned data, and tools like OKF export so your character's knowledge stays portable and under your control. When the code is public, trust isn't something we ask for — it's something you can check for yourself.
