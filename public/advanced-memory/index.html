Advanced Memory That Learns

Your Clanker AI characters don't just chat — they remember. Every conversation, uploaded document, and web search becomes durable knowledge, automatically organized into a local-first wiki that keeps your character consistent, accurate, and always improving.

Three ways your character learns

💬

From conversation

As you talk, your character extracts and stores the facts that matter — names, preferences, decisions, and history — so it remembers them in every future chat.

📄

From your documents

Upload PDFs, Word docs, and images. Your character builds foundational knowledge from them and keeps evolving as you add more source material.

🌐

From live web search

Your character can search the web mid-conversation and fold what it finds into its growing knowledge base — even during a live voice call.

A knowledge wiki, not a prompt dump

Most AI assistants forget everything the moment a conversation ends, or bloat every prompt with a wall of pasted history. Clanker AI is different. Its local-first LLM Wiki engine organizes what your character learns into structured facts, tasks, relationships, and a timeline — then actively reconciles conflicting information so the memory stays consistent and accurate over time, without prompt bloat.

Your memory, fully portable

Because it's built on structured knowledge, your character's entire memory can be exported with OKF — the Open Knowledge Format introduced by Google Cloud. Back it up, read and edit it in a Markdown editor like Obsidian, restore it, or clone a whole new character from it. You own your data.
