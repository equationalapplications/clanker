Images On Demand, Straight from Your Character

Working on something? Ask your character for a chart, a diagram, or a quick mockup without ever leaving the conversation. Not working? Ask for a selfie. If you can describe it, your character can draw it — right inside your chat.

Try the App

How it works

  1. Ask — or accept the offer. Request an image in chat, or accept when your character says "want me to draw that?". Both triggers generate. An offer alone never spends credits.
  2. The image arrives in the thread. Up to one image per reply, rendered inline in the chat bubble alongside the text.
  3. Save or share from the viewer. Tap the image to open the viewer, then share it or save it to your camera roll (Save to Photos is available in the native app). The image also lands in that character's gallery.

Save it, share it, keep it

Share is built into the image viewer on every platform, and Save to Photos is built in for the native app, so anything your character draws is one tap away from your group chat or (in the native app) your camera roll. Every generated image is also saved to that character's image gallery, so nothing gets lost in scrollback. Whatever your character builds stays with it — see how memory works.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does my character make an image?

When you ask in chat — or when you accept its offer to draw something. Your character can offer an image unprompted, but only generates one when you ask or explicitly accept.

How many images per reply?

Up to one image per reply.

Can I save or share what it makes?

Yes — Share is built into the image viewer on every platform, and Save to Photos is available in the native app. Every generated image is also saved to that character's image gallery.
